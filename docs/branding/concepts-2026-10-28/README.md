## Recommendations

**Margin 02, The held space**, is the strongest starting point: its open enclosure balances privacy with incoming discoveries, its bold geometry reads at small sizes, and it does not depend on an M monogram. Its simple frame-and-dot vocabulary is common, so distinctiveness and spacing need further testing. **Margin 04, The annotation** is a warmer, more editorial runner-up; its bracket could be mistaken for punctuation and the mark may feel less specific to mixed media.

**Nook 08** is the most inviting alternative: the alcove conveys a personal place without suggesting a particular medium. Nook is a familiar, crowded word with established associations, so naming clearance may be difficult. **Tuck 11** is short and active, and the pocket expresses putting something aside; it can sound more like an action than the name of a lasting collection. Sift has a clear discovery connection but emphasizes filtering over keeping. Folio is highly generic and leans toward documents; Morrow is evocative but implies postponement; Vela is pronounceable but its meaning and sailing association are less immediately connected to the product.

These are creative directions, not name-clearance advice. Domain and trademark availability have not been checked. Before choosing, test the leading marks against real app navigation, extension chrome, and neighboring products, then commission or refine a consistent wordmark.
