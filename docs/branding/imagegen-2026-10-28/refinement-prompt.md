# Current-logo refinement prompt

Generated with the built-in `image_gen.imagegen` tool, `transparent_background: false`. Input: [the current logo rendered from the app](current-logo-reference.png).

The output is an optical comparison, with intended changes rather than pixel-exact SVG measurements. Production changes can use the exact SVG/CSS values after a direction is selected.

```text
Use case: logo-brand, precise refinement of an existing identity.
Input image: the supplied image is the CURRENT, approved Margin logo, shown in dark and light themes. Treat it as the exact visual reference. Create ONE polished comparison sheet showing this same identity with only small optical refinements. This is not a rebrand.

Preserve the lowercase "margin" wordmark's font, weight, kerning and proportions exactly as closely as possible. Preserve its green period. Preserve the recognizable thin-line M symbol: two vertical outer legs, inward diagonals meeting at the center, short outward top terminals with rounded caps, and a SEPARATE short vertical stroke underneath the center. That center stroke must stay detached from the diagonal valley. No new symbols, leaf shapes, book icons, script fonts, serif wordmarks, or replacement monograms.

Composition: four horizontal rows, two columns. Left column dark warm charcoal/green as in the reference, ivory lettering and mint period. Right column warm ivory as in the reference, dark-green lettering and green period. Identical lockup scale and alignment in all eight cells, plenty of breathing room. Add discreet, clearly readable row captions only:
"A  Current"
"B  Stronger mark"
"C  Weight + spacing"
"D  Shorter center"

Row A: reproduce the supplied current logo faithfully.
Row B: keep A unchanged except increase ONLY the M symbol's stroke thickness by approximately 10 percent. Do not make the wordmark bolder.
Row C: use B's subtly stronger symbol and increase the gap between symbol and wordmark from roughly 6 to 8 relative units. Keep the lettering unchanged.
Row D: use C, but shorten the detached lower central stroke by about 25 percent, leaving a clear gap between it and the diagonal valley, and keeping it visible. Leave the rest of the symbol untouched.

The visual differences should be subtle and controlled, so a person can choose a refinement of the familiar current logo. The wordmark text must remain exactly "margin." everywhere. Produce a crisp, high-resolution brand comparison image with accurate readable captions, clean flat colors, and consistent typography. No mockup devices, no stationery, no gradients, no shadows, no invented illustrations, no explanatory prose.
```
