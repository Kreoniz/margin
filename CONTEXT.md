# Margin

Margin helps its owner choose what to read, watch, or play next from saved items, and find those items again later.

## Language

**Library**:
The owner's saved items, including unfinished and completed items. Saving an item does not commit the owner to consuming it next.

**Up next**:
A deliberately chosen, ordered selection of unfinished library items. Removing an item from Up next keeps it in the library; completing it removes it from Up next.
_Avoid_: Priority score, deadline

**Done**:
An item the owner has marked finished. Returning it to unfinished does not by itself express a new priority.

**Discover**:
Articles collected from followed sources that the owner can choose to save to the library.

**Needs attention**:
A concrete problem with a saved item's identity or details that the owner may choose to correct. Deliberately saving an item does not itself create a review obligation.
