Your shortcuts

Open capture, then press S to save or D to mark done. H/L and arrow keys move between the actions. These letter keys work inside the popup.

Direct Save and Done browser shortcuts are optional. If a shortcut is unassigned or taken, choose one in your browser’s extension settings.

New captures are private. Review details, change visibility, or revoke the browser token in Margin.
